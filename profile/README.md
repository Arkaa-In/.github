## Philosophy

Traditional systems ask you to **trust** them with your data. We build systems where trust is unnecessary — where the math does the work.

- **Zero-knowledge proofs** — reveal nothing beyond what is required
- **Privacy by default** — not an opt-in feature
- **Open source** — every claim is auditable
